Fit Nova is built with these open-source assets. Thank you to their authors.

- Phosphor Icons (MIT) - icons
- Fluent Emoji Flat by Microsoft (MIT) - colourful artwork
- GitLab SVGs, Pajamas illustrations (MIT) - small scenes
- Open Doodles by Pablo Stanley (CC0) - people illustrations
- Manrope (SIL Open Font License 1.1) - the font
- Photos from Wikimedia Commons - see "Photos" at the end

Icons, artwork and illustrations were converted to Android vector drawables and recoloured.
